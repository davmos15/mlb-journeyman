CAREER RECORD

How to play

A mystery player's career record is revealed one season at a time, in a random order.

Every wrong guess reveals another season of their career. Fewer seasons, more bragging rights.

1998  ■ NYY  149  .324  19  84  127  30  .842

A hitter's season shows the team, games, batting average, home runs, RBI, runs, stolen bases and OPS.

1999  ■ BOS  31  23  4  2.07  213.1  313  0.92

If the mystery player is a pitcher, the table switches to a pitching line: games, wins, losses, ERA, innings, strikeouts and WHIP. Tap any column heading — or a team code — for what it means.

After each wrong guess you also get clue chips comparing your guess to the mystery player: whether they shared a team, whether they played the same position, and whether the answer debuted earlier or later than your guess.

Normal: 8 guesses. Hard mode: 5 guesses, teams hidden, no clue chips.

Daily All-Time and per-decade games, or hit Practice for unlimited random players.

About
